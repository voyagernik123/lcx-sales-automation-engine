THE ASSET (public/objects/forge.glb, 160,520 B + .render.json)
  · scripts/blender/export_gltf.py writes the GLB BY HAND — no exporter add-on, no Draco: KHR_mesh_quantization from the start
    (int16 positions in the unit cube with the extent on the node, int8 normals, uint16 uv/indices). Blender 5.2.0 LTS.
  · disc/ring/plinth are build_forge.py's scene with bevel modifiers; the LCX mark is a boolean difference of the EXTRACTED
    path data in apps/web/public/lcx-mark.svg (never redrawn) — 4 polygons, 0.012 deep; 72 vertices sit at the cut floor.
    The room marker is a fourth mesh (r 0.18, h 0.05, bevelled). 12,376 triangles in all; ring slimmed 160×24 → 128×16
    (tube facets 0.02 units, below a device pixel at render scale).
  · Y-UP. The first export lay on its side: Blender is Z-up, the engine Y-up (mesh.ts: a cylinder's axis is y). The exporter
    carries the forward map (x, y, z) → (x, z, −y) on positions and normals; bounds now equal the live radii on every axis.
  · Brand hex FROM THE BYTES: oneObject.test parses the glb and encodes the ring's linear baseColorFactor back through the
    engine's own sRGB transfer — #2C6BFF exactly — and holds the sidecar (bytes = file, Standard, engraving polygons ≥ 1).

THE ENGINE
  · packages/gl/src/env/gltf.ts — GLB container, accessors decoded generically by componentType + normalized (quantized files
    take the float path), node TRS applied (de-quantization lives on the node), NORMAL/TANGENT derived when absent, pbr
    factors + our extras. Refusals in words (bad magic, short file, external buffers, unsupported required extension,
    non-indexed or non-triangle primitive, index out of range, node hierarchy). 5 tests. Exported from the index.
  · stageScene.slabGeometry: OUTWARD 45° chamfer (PLATE_CHAMFER 0.05) — outward so the highlight sits in the gutter beside
    the page, never under a glyph (the plate ceiling is about the top face). Faces oriented outward BY CONSTRUCTION, checked
    against the centroid under computeNormals. 4 tests.

THE WIRING
  · ForgeBackdrop draws the four primitives on its FIRST frame (nothing waits on a fetch), then fetches the glb and swaps
    disc/ring/plinth in place through forgeObjects.ts — every named mesh present and every upload accepted, or nothing is
    swapped and what was uploaded is disposed (3 tests). Materials stay in the component, per theme (anisoPreserved pins
    them). The outcome is written on the canvas (`data-objects`) for the instrument. `layer="cover"` lays the same object
    over the /lcxos still with build_forge.py's camera (target 0.34, distance 5, az 22°, el 24°, fov 30°). print:hidden.
  · Stage.tsx fetches the same file (browser-cached after the first route) and draws the marker under each room glow;
    `data-markers` records the outcome; absent for good if it refuses — the glows alone are the P3 frame.
  · No eager bytes: the fetch begins after the first frame is scheduled; index.html preloads nothing new.
